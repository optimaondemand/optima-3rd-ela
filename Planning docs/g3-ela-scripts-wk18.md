**Card: street** · `assets/audio/guide/opti/g3ela-18-1-card-street.mp3` · 40 words

## 18.2 · `lesson-18-2-wb.html`

**Opti’s welcome (Warm-Up, under the greeting)** · `assets/audio/guide/opti/g3ela-18-2-welcome.mp3` · 60 words

> Hi, it’s Opti. Last time you met a prince who never gets punished and a boy who always does. Today the prince has a big idea, and it isn’t a good one. As you read, ask yourself: is being bored a good enough reason to drag someone else into danger? Then you’ll give your opinion a reason to stand on.

> Word work first. Today you’ll match some long words to what they mean, and you’ll go hunting for joining words inside sentences. A joining word never comes alone. It brings a whole chunk of the sentence along with it. See if you can catch the word and its chunk together.

**Opti · before you write (top of Assignment)** · `assets/audio/guide/opti/g3ela-18-2-3.mp3` · 46 words

> Yesterday you wrote a claim. Today you give it a reason, the because behind your opinion. A strong reason comes from something a character really did, not just how you feel. Look back at today’s chapters and pick the one moment that proves your point best.
