Reading: How a Character Changes
3 points
At the beginning of the story, Despereaux was an odd little mouse who was banished because he dared to love a princess. At the end, he walked through the darkness and rescued her. He still loved music and light — but now he also knew what it meant to be truly brave.
— Adapted from The Tale of Despereaux, Book Four
Beginning vs. End 1 pt
10How was Despereaux different at the end of the story compared to the beginning? Use a detail from the passage.
Character Change 1 pt
11What event in the story do you think changed Despereaux the most? Explain what happened and why it mattered.
Theme Connection 1 pt
12How does Despereaux's change connect to a theme of the book? Write a sentence naming the theme and explaining how his change shows it.
