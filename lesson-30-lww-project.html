Welcome to the Lamp-Post Museum

A museum keeps important objects safe and tells their stories. Today you are the curator, the person in charge of the museum. You will choose three objects from Narnia and write a label for each one. Then you will write a note about what the ending of the book shows. Your work saves as you go.

How your project is graded (12 points)
4 · Exceeds3 · Meets2 · Approaching1 · Beginning

Why it matters (labels)All three labels give a clear reason the object matters and connect it to a big idea, like honesty or forgivenessAll three labels give a clear reason the object mattersOne or two labels give a reasonNo reasons
Curator’s Note and writingTells what the ending shows, with a detail from the book and one of your objects; capitals and end marks are rightTells what the ending shows, with a detail from the book; a few small mistakesTells what the ending shows with no detail, or mistakes make it hard to readThe note is missing
Each label has four parts: the object’s name, a quote from the book, what happens, and why it matters.
