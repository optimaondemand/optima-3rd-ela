📖 This Week's Passage
From Chapter 9 — In the Library
Despereaux was braver than anyone expected. Roscuro was more broken than anyone knew. The Princess Pea was kinder than anyone deserved. And Miggery Sow — who was sold for a tablecloth, a hen, and a red bowl — wanted more than anyone had ever thought to give her. In the end, they were all, in their own way, more than the world had made room for. That is the whole point of a story. To make room.

Four characters, each described in one sentence. Read each like a portrait being placed on a wall. Then let the last two sentences — “To make room.” — feel like a conclusion you’ve been working toward all year.

2
🎯 The 4 Steps
Work through these in order.
1️⃣

Read for accuracy. Read the passage out loud. Say every word correctly. Slow down where you need to.

1️⃣

Read for smoothness. Read it again. This time, try not to pause or stumble. Let the sentences flow.

2️⃣

Read with expression. Read it a third time. Let “To make room.” land alone, quietly, at the very end — like the final note of a piece of music.

3️⃣

Record and submit. Use Canvas's recording tool to record your final reading. Upload it to this assignment.

3
🤔 Self-Check
Before you submit your recording.
Ask yourself:

Did I say every word correctly?

Did my reading sound smooth?

Did my voice show the feeling of the passage?

🎙️

If you can answer "yes" to all three, you're ready to submit. If not, read it once more.
