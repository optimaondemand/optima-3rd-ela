🛟Having trouble in VR?Steps with pictures, and a how-to video

How-to video: Optima On-Demand and Canvas

These pictures come from other maps. Your map will be {{MAP}}, but the buttons are the same.

🖥️ First time only: connect Canvas (on your computer)

  1. 1Log in

    Go to xr.optimaed.com. Log in the same way you do in VR.

  2. 2Connect to Canvas

    Click Connect to Canvas. Log in to Canvas, then click Authorize.

  3. 3Find our class

    Click Canvas on the left and pick our class. You only do this one time.

📄 Only if the assignment has a file

  1. 4Get the file ready

    Click Open in Canvas to download the file. Then click My Files and Upload File.

🥽 In the headset

  1. 5Explore tab

    Click Explore. Type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 6Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo.

  3. 7Read the assignment

    Press the top button on your right controller. Click Canvas and pick our assignment.

  4. 8Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type the name, then click a model to bring it out.

  5. 9Move and resize

    Grab with the palm trigger. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  6. 10Need a file?

    Click Library, then Files, and pick your file.

  7. 11Take a photo

    Click Library, then Camera. Make it all fit, then click Take photo.

  8. 12Pick your photo

    Click Canvas and pick our assignment. Click Choose from My Files, pick your newest photo, then Use this file.

  9. 13Submit to Canvas

    Click Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag. To leave, press the Meta button, then Quit.

🔁 Didn’t work in VR? Two other ways

  1. 14Use the website

    Go to xr.optimaed.com and click Canvas. Pick our assignment, click Choose from My Files, and submit your photo.

Your photos also save to My Files at xr.optimaed.com. You can download your photo there and upload it to the Canvas assignment yourself.
