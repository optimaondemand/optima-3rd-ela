The broadsides got it wrong

Remember the news sellers at the fair? Their broadsides said Jemmy was the rascal. That was wrong! First you’ll make a true Wanted poster for the real outlaws, Hold-Your-Nose Billy and Cutwater. Then you’ll write Jemmy’s letter to the king, telling what really happened. Use what you know from the book. Your work saves as you go.
