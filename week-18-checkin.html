Reading
3 points — Character Introduction
The prince was known throughout the kingdom as Prince Brat. He had never been whipped in his life, for it was the law: no one was allowed to strike a prince. Jemmy had been brought to the castle to take his punishments for him. Jemmy had learned to read and write from listening at doorways and peering at books. Prince Brat had learned nothing at all.
— Adapted from The Whipping Boy, Ch. 1–2
10Reading
How are Jemmy and Prince Brat different, according to this passage? Use a detail from the text.
11Reading
Why do you think Jemmy learned to read and write, even though he wasn't taught? What does this tell you about him?
12Reading
Does it seem fair that Jemmy has to take the prince's punishments? Use details from the passage to explain your thinking.
