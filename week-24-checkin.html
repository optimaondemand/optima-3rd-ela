Gulliver's Stories — Part 3: Strange Lands
📅 Week 24 · Check-In

This week Gulliver visited more unusual places with strange inventions and customs. You studied main idea and supporting details in paragraphs, the root script/scribe (meaning "write"), and the suffixes -ful, -less, -ness, -ly. Show what you know!

Section 1: Grammar — Main Idea and Details in Paragraphs
3 points
1 Multiple Choice
Which sentence is a strong main idea sentence for a paragraph about Gulliver's unusual adventures?
  • A Gulliver saw a floating island.
  • B Gulliver's travels took him to many strange and unusual places that changed how he saw the world.
  • C The people on the island wore strange clothes.
2 Fix-It Sentence
Rewrite the sentence below so it flows more clearly and correctly.
"Gulliver visited a land where the scientists worked on silly projects they thought very important."
3 Write Your Own
Write a main idea sentence for a paragraph about one place Gulliver visited. Then write one supporting detail sentence.

Section 2: Morphology — Root script / scribe (write)
3 points
4 Root Meaning
The root script/scribe means "write." Think about the word describe. What does describe mean? How does knowing scribe help you figure it out?
5 Multiple Choice — Word in Context
"Gulliver noticed an inscription carved into the stone wall — words that had been written there long ago by someone who wanted to leave a message."
Adapted from Gulliver's Stories, Part 3
What does inscription most likely mean?
  • A A picture drawn in bright colors
  • B Words or letters written or carved onto something
  • C A type of unusual building material
6 Apply It
Think of one more word with the root script or scribe (like manuscript, prescription, or transcript). Write it and explain how "write" connects to its meaning.

Section 4: Reading — Explain How Details Support Meaning
3 points
On one island Gulliver visited, the scientists spent all their time on strange experiments that never worked. One man tried to grow sunlight out of cucumbers, and another tried to build houses from the roof down instead of from the ground up. Gulliver watched quietly, thinking about how different their way of solving problems was from anything he had ever seen.
Adapted from Gulliver's Stories, Part 3
10 Main Idea
What is the main idea of this passage? Write it in your own words.
11 Supporting Details
What details does the author use to show that the scientists' ideas are unusual? Name at least two details from the passage.
12 Author's Purpose
Why do you think the author includes such absurd details about the scientists' experiments? What might the author be trying to say about how people think about problems?
✏
Section 5: Writing & Read-Write-Mean
2 points
13 Read-Write-Mean (RWM)
📚 Model Sentence
"Gulliver visited a land of dreamers and tinkerers — people full of clever ideas that never quite worked, but who were never hopeless enough to give up trying."
Notice how the author adds facts and details to paint a vivid picture of a place or group of people. Write your own sentence describing an imaginary place or group of people using at least two specific details.
14 Writing Arc — Draft Body Paragraph
Write a short paragraph (4–5 sentences) about one of Gulliver's adventures. Include: a main idea sentence, at least two supporting detail sentences, and a closing sentence. Use details from this week's reading.
