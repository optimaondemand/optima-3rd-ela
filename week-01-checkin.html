Reading: Preview & Ask Questions
3 points
One morning, Stanley Lambchop woke up to find that the big bulletin board had fallen on him during the night. He was now only half an inch thick — but otherwise perfectly fine. His mother measured him carefully. His father looked worried. But Stanley was not worried at all. He just wanted to know what happened next.
— Adapted from Flat Stanley, Ch. 1 summary
Cite a Detail 1 pt
10What happened to Stanley while he slept? Use one detail from the passage in your answer.
Ask a Question 1 pt
11Good readers ask questions before and while they read. Write one question you have about Stanley after reading this passage.
Predict 1 pt
12What do you predict might be one good thing and one hard thing about being flat like Stanley? Write one sentence for each.

Writing & RWM Transfer
2 points
Read Like a Writer — Mimic Sentence 1 pt
Model Sentence
"Stanley was only half an inch thick, but he smiled and decided everything would be all right."
Notice the pattern: the writer tells something surprising about a character, then shows how the character feels or responds. Write your own sentence using this same pattern about a real or made-up character.
Pattern: [Character] was [something surprising], but [they] [felt/decided/did something].
