Grammar 3 pts
Irregular Verbs

Morphology 3 pts
Prefix com- / con- (with, together)
4 Word-Part Breakdown
Look at the word connect. The prefix con- means “with” or “together.” What does “connect” mean? Use the prefix meaning in your explanation.
5 Multiple Choice
Which word uses the prefix com- or con- correctly to mean “with” or “together”?
  • A combine — to mix things together
  • B confuse — to go back in time
  • C corner — to make something come apart

Suffixes -ful and -less

8 Explain the Pattern

Reading 3 pts
Track Chronology in Biography
First, Joan convinced the prince to give her soldiers and armor. Next, she led her army to a city called Orléans, which had been surrounded by enemies for a long time. After many days of hard fighting, Joan and her soldiers freed the city. Finally, the people of France cheered because they believed Joan had been sent by God to save them.
Adapted from Joan of Arc by Diane Stanley
