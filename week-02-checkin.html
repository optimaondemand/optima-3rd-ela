Reading: Character, Setting, and Problem
3 points
Stanley Lambchop was a cheerful boy who lived with his parents and his younger brother Arthur. One morning, everything changed: the big bulletin board above Stanley's bed had fallen on him during the night, and now he was as flat as a pancake. His family was worried, but Stanley took it in stride. He could slide under doors, roll up like a poster, and be mailed in an envelope — and he thought most of these things were pretty interesting.
— Adapted from Flat Stanley, Chapters 1–2
Character 1 pt

Inference 1 pt

Read Like a Writer — Mimic Sentence 1 pt
Model Sentence
"He was a cheerful boy with a wide smile and a very flat shadow."
Notice the pattern: the writer introduces a character with a describing word, then adds two specific details with "and." Write your own sentence introducing a character — real or made up — using this same pattern.
Pattern: [Name] was a [describing word] [noun] with [detail] and [detail].
