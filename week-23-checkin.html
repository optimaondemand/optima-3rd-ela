Gulliver's Stories — Part 2: The Land of Giants
📅 Week 23 · Check-In

This week Gulliver traveled to a land of giants, where everything was enormous and he was the tiny one. You studied verb tense consistency, the prefix trans- (meaning "across"), and syllable division with the VCCV pattern. Show what you know!

✎
Section 1: Grammar — Verb Tense Consistency
3 points
1 Multiple Choice
Which sentence keeps verb tenses consistent?
  • A Gulliver arrived in the giant land and sees a huge farmhouse.
  • B Gulliver arrived in the giant land and saw a huge farmhouse.
  • C Gulliver will arrive and had seen a huge farmhouse.
2 Fix-It Sentence
Rewrite the sentence below so all verb tenses are consistent (all past tense).
"The giant farmer finds Gulliver in the field and picks him up, and then he walked back to his house."
3 Write Your Own
Write two sentences about Gulliver's adventure in the land of giants. Make sure both sentences use past tense verbs.
📖
Section 2: Morphology — Prefix trans- (across)
3 points
4 Prefix Meaning
The prefix trans- means "across." Think about the word transport. What does transport mean? How does knowing trans- help you figure it out?
5 Multiple Choice — Word in Context
"The giant carefully decided to transfer Gulliver from one hand to the other, moving him across from left to right without dropping him."
Adapted from Gulliver's Stories, Part 2
What does transfer most likely mean?
  • A To leave something in the same place
  • B To move something from one place to another
  • C To break something into many pieces
6 Apply It
Write a sentence using the word translate and explain how the prefix trans- (across) connects to its meaning.
✍
Section 3: Spelling — Syllable Division VCCV
3 points
7 Divide the Syllables
In VCCV words, divide between the two consonants (like hap-pen or but-ter). Divide each word into syllables. Write each word with a slash between syllables.
happen butter dinner rabbit sudden
8 Explain the Pattern
Explain the VCCV pattern in your own words. Where do you divide a VCCV word? Why is knowing this pattern helpful when you read?
9 Spot the Error
One word has been divided incorrectly. Find it and write the correct syllable division.
ham-mer win-ter butto-n

Section 4: Reading — Summarize Unusual Events Clearly
3 points
In the land of the giants, Gulliver was so small that a common mouse was the size of an elephant to him. The farmer's daughter took care of Gulliver like a doll, making him a tiny bed and bringing him scraps of food. Everything Gulliver had ever thought was normal now seemed strange and upside down.
Adapted from Gulliver's Stories, Part 2
10 Summarize
In your own words, summarize what happens to Gulliver in the land of giants. Use at least two details from the passage.
11 Author's Comparisons
How does the author help you understand how different this land is from Gulliver's home? What comparisons does the author use?
12 Think It Through
Think about what it would feel like to be smaller than everyone and everything around you. How might that change the way you see the world? Use a detail from the passage to support your thinking.
✏
Section 5: Writing & Read-Write-Mean
2 points
13 Read-Write-Mean (RWM)
📚 Model Sentence
"First, Gulliver had been the giant among tiny people; now, he was the tiny one among giants — and the world looked completely different from down below."
Notice how the author uses sequence and contrast to connect events. Write your own sentence that uses sequence words (first, then, finally, now) to explain how a situation changed.
14 Writing Arc — Organized Notes
You are gathering information for an expository piece about Gulliver's travels. List 3 facts or details from this week's reading that you could use in a paragraph about the land of giants. Write each fact as a short note.
