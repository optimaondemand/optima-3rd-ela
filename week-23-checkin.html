Medieval Nonfiction — Life in a Castle
📋Week 23 · Check-In

This week you explored nonfiction texts about life in a medieval castle — who lived there, what their jobs were, and how they spent their days. You practiced capitalization of titles, names, and places, and the root struct (meaning "build"). Show what you know!

Reading
3 points — Main Idea and Key Details
A medieval castle was home to many different people — not just the king and his family. Knights, servants, cooks, guards, and stablehands all lived within the castle walls. Each person had a job to do. The cook prepared meals in the great kitchen. The steward managed the household. The guards walked the battlements day and night, watching for enemies.
— Medieval Nonfiction Extension, Week 23
