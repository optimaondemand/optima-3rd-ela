Section 1: Grammar — Complex Sentences

1 Multiple Choice · 1 pt
Which sentence is a complex sentence? (A complex sentence has an independent clause and a dependent clause connected by a subordinating conjunction.)
  • A Aslan is a great lion.
  • B The children ran and played in the meadow.
  • C Although the winter had lasted many years, flowers began to appear when Aslan arrived.

Section 2: Morphology — Root port (carry)
3 points
4 Written Response · 1 pt
The root port means “carry.” Think about the word transport. What does transport mean? How does knowing port help you understand it?
5 Multiple Choice · 1 pt
Read the sentence. What does portable most likely mean?
“Aslan carried a portable throne with him so he could hold meetings anywhere in Narnia — it was easy to carry from place to place.”
  • A Very large and impossible to move
  • B Able to be carried from place to place
  • C Made entirely of solid gold

Section 4: Reading — Notice Symbolism and Repeated Ideas
3 points
As Aslan walked through Narnia, something strange began to happen. The ice that had covered the land for one hundred years cracked and dripped. Snowdrops pushed through the mud, and birds began to sing for the first time in memory. It was as if Narnia itself knew that the long winter was finally ending.
Adapted from The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, Ch. 11 — C.S. Lewis
10 Written Response · 1 pt
What is happening in Narnia as Aslan arrives? Describe it using details from the passage.
11 Written Response · 1 pt
Authors sometimes use nature to represent something bigger, like hope or change. What might the melting snow and blooming flowers represent in this story? Explain your thinking.
12 Written Response · 1 pt
The author repeats the idea of “winter ending” several times in the story. Why do you think C.S. Lewis keeps coming back to this idea? What is he trying to show the reader?
