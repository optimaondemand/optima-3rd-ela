Reading passage (summary of Ch. 12–13)

Jemmy burrowed under the moldy straw in the outlaws’ hut, waiting for his chance to slip away. He asked the prince to tell Cutwater that he had slipped out the door. But when Cutwater asked where the prince was, Prince Brat answered right away: “Him? Over there. Under the straw.” Jemmy burst out of the straw and ran for his life, with Cutwater close behind. But this, he had not expected: when he finally stopped running, the prince had followed him.
