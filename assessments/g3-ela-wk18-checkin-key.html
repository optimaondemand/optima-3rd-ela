Reading passage (summary of Ch. 1–2)

The prince was known throughout the kingdom as Prince Brat. He had never been whipped in his life, for it was the law: no one was allowed to strike a prince. Jemmy had been brought to the castle to take his punishments for him. Jemmy had to sit close at hand during the prince’s daily lessons. The prince learned nothing — but Jemmy learned to read, write, and do sums.
