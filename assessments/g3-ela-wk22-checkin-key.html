Reading passage (from Chapter 17)

Jemmy watched the prince slowly raise himself off the mud flat. He felt a growing amazement. … There was a cast-iron streak of pluck in him.
“Steady on your legs?” Jemmy asked.
“Steady.”
… “Does everyone hate me?”
“More’n likely.”
“What about you?”
Jemmy hesitated for a moment. “I did. But maybe I don’t.” Jemmy couldn’t sort out his feelings.
