Reading passage (summary of Ch. 16)

Hold-Your-Nose Billy thought the prince was only the whipping boy, so he raised Captain Nips’s horsewhip and brought it down on the prince’s back. Jemmy had dreamed of seeing the prince whipped, but now he found no satisfaction in it. “Bawl out!” he shouted. The prince set his jaw, just as Jemmy always had, and not a sound escaped his lips. He only got ready for the next blow.
