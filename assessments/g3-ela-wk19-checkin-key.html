Reading passage (summary of Ch. 7–8)

Hold-Your-Nose Billy watched the two boys closely. The ragged whipping boy knew his letters, but the boy who called himself a prince could not even sign his own name. “Something’s amiss here,” Billy said. He decided Jemmy must be the real prince and made plans to trade him to the king for a ransom of gold. The real prince shouted that he was His Royal Highness, but Billy told him to hush. For the first time, being royal didn’t seem to be any advantage at all.
