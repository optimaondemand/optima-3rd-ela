Reading passage (The 7 Pillars of Virtue, written for Optima Academy Online)

The 7 Pillars of Virtue are honesty, respect, courtesy, responsibility, courage, service and perseverance. Courage means acting even when you are afraid, because something matters more than your fear. Service means helping others without expecting a reward. Perseverance means continuing to try even when things are hard.
