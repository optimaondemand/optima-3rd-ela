This week you began reading The Tale of Despereaux and met an unusual little mouse who loves music and light. You studied common and proper nouns, the suffix -ing, and adding -ed/-ing to base words. Show what you know!

Grammar: Common & Proper Nouns
3 points
Identification 1 pt
1Which sentence uses a proper noun correctly?
  • AThe little mouse was born in a Castle near France.
  • BDespereaux was a mouse who loved music and light.
  • CThe Princess gave the mouse a Book to read.
Correction 1 pt
2Fix the capitalization in this sentence. Write the corrected sentence.
the mouse named despereaux lived in a castle in the kingdom of dor.
Application 1 pt
3Write a sentence about Despereaux using at least one common noun and one proper noun. Label each one.
🔬
Morphology: Suffix -ing
3 points
Word-Part Breakdown 1 pt
4Look at the word listening. What is the base word? How does adding -ing change the word?
Context Clues 1 pt
5Read the sentence. What does trembling most likely mean?
Despereaux stood before the king, trembling from the tips of his ears to his tiny pink toes — his whole body was shaking with fear.
— Adapted from The Tale of Despereaux, Book One
  • ALaughing loudly and happily
  • BShaking because of fear or cold
  • CJumping up and down with excitement
Use It 1 pt
6Add -ing to the word wonder to make a new word. Then write a sentence using that new word.
🔤
Spelling: Adding -ed and -ing to Base Words
3 points
Sort 1 pt
7Add -ed and -ing to each base word. Write both new words.
look help walk
Explain the Pattern 1 pt
8When you add -ed or -ing to a base word like jump, you do NOT change the spelling. In your own words, explain why these base words are easy to add endings to.
Spelling Fix 1 pt
9One of these words is spelled incorrectly. Find it and write the correct spelling.

Reading: Notice How a Character Is Introduced
3 points
Despereaux was a very small mouse with very large ears. He was born with his eyes open, which was strange. From the start, he did things that no other mouse would do — he listened to music, he gazed at light, and he was not afraid of things that should have frightened him.
— Adapted from The Tale of Despereaux, Ch. 1–2
Cite a Detail 1 pt
10What is one unusual thing about Despereaux? Use a detail from the passage in your answer.
Character Introduction 1 pt
11Authors introduce characters by showing what they look like AND what they do. What does the author show about Despereaux in this passage? Give one detail about his looks and one about his actions.
Infer 1 pt
12Based on how the author introduces Despereaux, what kind of character do you think he will be in the story? Use at least one detail from the passage to support your answer.
✍️
Writing & RWM Transfer
2 points
Read Like a Writer — Mimic Sentence 1 pt
Model Sentence (Show Don't Tell)
"He had large ears that trembled whenever he heard beautiful music, and his eyes went wide and bright as if the light itself were talking to him."
13Notice how the author SHOWS us that Despereaux loves music and light — instead of just saying "he loved them." Write your own sentence that SHOWS something about a character without using the word "felt," "was," or "loved."
Tip: Show it through what the character does with their body or face!
Writing Arc — Fictional Narrative 1 pt
14This quarter you will write a fictional narrative! This week, brainstorm an unusual character — like Despereaux is an unusual mouse. Describe your character in 1–2 sentences. What makes them different or surprising?
