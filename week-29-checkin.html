Reading 3 pts
Central Idea and Supporting Details
Joan of Arc did not stop fighting even after she was wounded. Her central message was that France deserved to be free. She showed this by leading soldiers into battle, by refusing to give up when things were hard, and by encouraging the French prince to take his rightful place as king. Every detail of her story points back to one main idea: she believed her purpose was to save France.
Adapted from Joan of Arc by Diane Stanley
