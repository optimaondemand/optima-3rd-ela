Aslan did not struggle. He lay down on the cold stone as they shaved his mane and tied him with cords. He submitted to it all. When it was over, and the Witch had gone, a great stillness lay over the hilltop. Susan and Lucy crept close and lay their hands on the warm fur, and wept.
