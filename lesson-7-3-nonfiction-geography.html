✏️ Grammar: Commas in dates and places
Use / Apply the skill
👉 Example
Boston, Massachusetts — city, state

Always use a comma between a city and its state.

👉 Example
In the summer, many families visit Florida.

Today’s text introduces five regions of the United States. As you read, pay attention to the headings — they organize the whole passage.

📰 Interesting Places in the United States
The United States is a big country with many different kinds of places to visit. In the northeast, you can visit old cities like Boston, Massachusetts, where American history began. In the southeast, warm weather and beaches make states like Florida popular. The midwest has wide-open farmland and the Great Lakes — five huge lakes that hold a large share of the world’s fresh water. In the southwest, the Grand Canyon in Arizona drops over a mile deep into the earth. The west has deserts, mountains, and the Pacific Ocean. California alone has forests, deserts, mountains, and beaches all in one state. No matter where you travel in the United States, you will find something different — a new climate, new food, and new history to discover.
Key terms in this text: Northeast Southeast Midwest Southwest West

Writer’s Workshop: Week 7 · Travel Guide
Things to Do + Helpful Tips
✓ Plan Your Guide ✓ Draft Section 1 ✓ Draft Sections 2 & 3 ★ Publish!
🎯

Today’s focus: Today: DRAFT SECTIONS 2 & 3. Write ‘Things to Do’ and ‘Helpful Tips.’

👀 Watch How — Meet Clara, our example writer
BEFORE
Clara’s tips section: “Go in the morning. Bring stuff.”
AFTER
Clara’s Section 3:
Helpful Tips
The best time to visit Lakeside Park is in the morning, when it is cool and quiet. Bring a water bottle because there are no vending machines inside the park. If you visit in summer, bring bug spray — the mosquitoes near the lake can be fierce! Wear comfortable shoes because the walking paths are unpaved.

Notice: Clara’s tips are specific. She told WHY (cool and quiet), WHAT to bring (water bottle, bug spray), and WHAT to expect (unpaved paths). Vague tips like “bring stuff” don’t help readers.
