This Week at a Glance

Jemmy hides under the straw in the outlaws’ hut and asks the prince to say he slipped out the door. Instead, the prince tells Cutwater, “Under the straw.” Jemmy runs into the forest, hides in a hollow tree, and later argues with the prince, who follows him anyway. A girl named Betsy, out looking for her dancing bear, points them toward the river. On the mud flats Jemmy hunts for things to sell, and the prince starts to help. When Jemmy gets a ride in an old coach, he looks back, sees the prince standing alone, and has the coach stop for him. Then two highwaymen block the road.

Reading skill
Reading between the lines. When a character doesn’t say how he feels, what he does shows it.
Big idea
Trust and change. The prince gives Jemmy away in Chapter 12. In Chapter 15, Jemmy goes back for him. What changed?

“Stop, Cap’n! We left my friend behind.”Jemmy, Chapter 15

Word parts
The root spect means “look.”inspect · spectator · respect · inspector
Spelling
Long words. Find the vowels, split between them, read each beat, then push the beats together. Bonus: c and g go soft before e, i, or y.im·por·tant · city · giant
Grammar
Prepositions sit in front of a noun and tell where, when, or how.under the straw · after the storm · with great care

New in the Lessons

🌲 The Chase
This week’s word game. Each right answer moves Jemmy one stone along a forest path, with Cutwater three stones behind. Eight stones a day, until Jemmy reaches the hollow tree on Day 4.
🎬 Opti’s videos
Every Word Study activity, all four days, starts with a short video where Opti shows how it works. On Day 2, Opti also shows how to do Read Like a Writer with a different sentence from the book.
🔍 Reading lens
Before reading, your student taps one of two lenses to use, like “What does he do?” It shows as a bookmark in the reading box.
🦁 Courage Watch
After reading, your student decides if three moments from the chapters show courage: Yes or Not yet.

Each day opens with a real old painting or print, like bear cubs in a pine forest and an old coach on the road. Your student looks first, then taps to learn about it.

Your Student’s Opinion Writing

Writer’s Workshop
Wk 18 · Choose a claimWk 19 · Add reasonsWk 20 · DraftWk 21 · Revise & publishWk 22 · Compare & present

This week your student polishes the opinion paragraph. Day 1: check that each example names a chapter. Day 2: end with a question or a challenge. Day 3: edit with a checklist (capitals, commas, names, end marks). Day 4: label all five parts (claim, reason, example, the other side, conclusion) and say what they are proud of.

An example writer named Clara shows each step first with her own opinion about Prince Brat.

Ask at Home

  1. What did the prince say when Cutwater asked where Jemmy was?Listen for: “Him? Over there. Under the straw.”
  2. How did Jemmy get away from Cutwater?Listen for: he burst out of the straw, ran into the forest, and hid in a hollow tree.
  3. What is mudlarking?Listen for: searching the river mud for things to sell, like driftwood.
  4. Why do you think Jemmy went back for the prince?Listen for: an opinion plus a reason from Chapter 15. Many answers can be right.
