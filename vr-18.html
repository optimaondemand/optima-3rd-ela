18.2 VR · The Runaways’ Ride
Opti · your guide

Hoo! Let’s step off the Reading Road and into the book. In Chapter 3, the prince wakes Jemmy in the night to run away. Build their ride in a forest, then take a photo for your teacher.

👀 What am I looking at?

This stop is a forest at night, from Chapters 3 and 4. The prince and Jemmy ride a castle horse into the trees.

🥽 In VR

1 Build the scene in VR → 2 Take a photo → 3 Submit it to Canvas
  1. Step 1 of 5 · Know your models

    These are your four models. Say each name out loud. This list is also in VR: open your menu, click the Canvas tab, then pick our assignment.

    • White Horse - Idletype: White Horsefrom the castle stable
    • BasketJemmy carries it. Make it big!
    • Cloakthe prince wears it
    • Moon Phases - Full Moontype: Full Moonit lit their way
  2. Step 2 of 5 · Go in

    🔗 First time only: connect Canvas at xr.optimaed.com first. See Having trouble in VR? below.Put on your headset and open the On-Demand app. Click the Explore tab. Type Forest in the Filter environments box and click Medieval Forest. Then click Begin Solo.

  3. Step 3 of 5 · Build it

    Press the top button on your right controller and click Models. Put the horse between the trees. Put the basket and the cloak next to it. Put the moon up high.

    ✋ To move a model: grab it with the palm trigger (the button under your middle finger). Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.
  4. Step 4 of 5 · Say it out loud

    Point to the horse and say this out loud:

    🗣 “The prince ran away because ___. He picked Jemmy because ___.”
  5. Step 5 of 5 · Snap and submit

    Stand back so you can see the horse, the basket, the cloak and the moon. Open your menu, click Library, then Camera. Move the tablet until it all fits, then click Take photo. Now click the Canvas tab and pick 18.2 VR · The Runaways’ Ride. Click Choose from My Files, pick your photo, click Use this file, then Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag. Last, press the Meta button and click Quit.

    🔁 Want to fix it? Take a new photo and submit again.
⭐ Extra stop (if you have time)

Add a Frog by the horse. Say why the prince once dumped bullfrogs in the moat.

⭐ Full points: Your photo shows the horse, the big basket, the cloak and the moon.

Road stamp earned! It’s on your Reading Road now.

🖥️ First time only: connect Canvas (on your computer)

  1. 1Log in

    Go to xr.optimaed.com. Log in the same way you do in VR.

  2. 2Connect to Canvas

    Click Connect to Canvas. Log in to Canvas, then click Authorize.

  3. 3Find our class

    Click Canvas on the left and pick our class. You only do this one time.

📄 Only if the assignment has a file

  1. 4Get the file ready

    Click Open in Canvas to download the file. Then click My Files and Upload File.

🥽 In the headset

  1. 5Explore tab

    Click Explore. Type the map’s name in Filter environments.

  2. 6Begin Solo

    Click the map, then click Begin Solo.

  3. 7Read the assignment

    Press the top button on your right controller. Click Canvas and pick our assignment.

  4. 8Bring out the models

    Click Models. Type the name, then click a model to bring it out.

  5. 9Move and resize

    Grab with the palm trigger. Use both palm triggers to make it bigger or smaller.

  6. 10Need a file?

    Click Library, then Files, and pick your file.

  7. 11Take a photo

    Click Library, then Camera. Make it all fit, then click Take photo.

  8. 12Pick your photo

    Click Canvas and pick our assignment. Click Choose from My Files, pick your newest photo, then Use this file.

  9. 13Submit to Canvas

    Click Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag. To leave, press the Meta button, then Quit.

🔁 Didn’t work in VR? Two other ways

  1. 14Use the website

    Go to xr.optimaed.com and click Canvas. Pick our assignment, click Choose from My Files, and submit your photo.

Your photos also save to My Files at xr.optimaed.com. You can download your photo there and upload it to the Canvas assignment yourself.
