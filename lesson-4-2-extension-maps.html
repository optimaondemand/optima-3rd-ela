✏️ Daily Oral Language
Study the model, then fix the sentence.

First, read the model sentence carefully. Notice what the author does right. Then, fix the second sentence using the same skills.

✅ Model Sentence — Study What's Right
One Sunday afternoon, in the street, they met Ralph Jones, an old college friend of Mr. Lambchop’s.

Notice: Sunday and Ralph Jones are proper nouns. Commas set off extra information. The apostrophe in Lambchop’s shows possession.

🔧 Fix-It Sentence — Now You Try
well george i see you have bought some wallpaper said mr jones going to decorate your house i suppose
📓 Write the corrected sentence

Corrected:

“Well, George, I see you have bought some wallpaper,” said Mr. Jones. “Going to decorate your house, I suppose?”
