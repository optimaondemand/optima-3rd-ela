Focus: Pause after the third line. Give each child's reaction a slightly different tone.

Mr Beaver leaned close and lowered his voice to a whisper.
The four children pressed in around him, waiting.
Then he said the name.
Peter felt suddenly brave, as if he had been waiting for this moment his whole life.
Susan felt something like music — faint and far away, but coming closer.
Lucy felt warm all the way through, the way you feel on the first real morning of summer.
Only Edmund felt nothing good at all — only a cold, hollow feeling, like a door shutting inside him.

Read it silently once. Then let’s look at the challenge words.

🎯
The 5-Step Fluency Routine
Work through these in order every time.
1️⃣

Read for accuracy. Say every word correctly. Slow down where needed.

1️⃣

Read for smoothness. Keep the flow going. Stumble? Back up one sentence and retry.

2️⃣

Read for expression. Let your voice match the feeling of the passage.

3️⃣

Record and submit. Record yourself reading and upload to Canvas.

🤔
Self-Check Before Submitting

Did I read every word correctly?

Did the reading flow without stopping?

Did my voice show the feeling of the passage?
