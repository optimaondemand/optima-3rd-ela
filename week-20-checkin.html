3 points
4 Written Response · 1 pt

5 Multiple Choice · 1 pt
Read the sentence. What does spectator most likely mean?
“A large crowd of Narnian creatures stood as spectators during the coronation — they watched from the sides of the great hall as Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy were crowned.”
  • A Someone who is performing on a stage
  • B Someone who watches an event
  • C Someone who builds things with their hands
