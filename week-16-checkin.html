Reading
3 points — Compare Two Characters
Despereaux wanted to be near the light — near the princess and her music and her kindness. Roscuro wanted the light too, but he had been in the darkness so long that he had forgotten how to simply ask for it. Both of them were searching for something. The difference was in what each chose to do about it.
— Adapted from The Tale of Despereaux
