Morphology: Suffix -est (most)
3 points
Word-Part Breakdown 1 pt

Context Clues 1 pt
5Read the sentence. What does cleverest most likely mean?
Of all the boys in the kingdom, Jemmy was the cleverest — no one could outthink him when it came to getting out of trouble and finding a way home.
— Adapted from The Whipping Boy, Ch. 18
  • Athe most clever or quick-thinking of all

Reading: Resolution and Theme
