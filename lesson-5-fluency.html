Weekly Fluency
A Passage from the Book

This is your weekly fluency assignment. Read the passage below several times — for accuracy, smoothness, and expression. Then record yourself reading it and submit your recording in Canvas.

🎯
Objective
By the end…
🎙️Fluency: I will read this passage accurately, smoothly, and with expression.
📖Standard: ELA.3.F.1.4 — Read grade-level texts with accuracy, automaticity, and prosody.
You'll need: 🎙️ A device that records 📕 Your Novel
1
📖 This Week's Passage
From Chapter 5 — Arthur’s Good Idea
“Thank you, Arthur,” Stanley said. “Thank you very much.” The brothers were shaking hands when Mr. Lambchop strode into the room with Mrs. Lambchop right behind him. “GEORGE!” said Mrs. Lambchop. “Stanley’s round again! And Arthur did it!”

Read Stanley’s words gently — he is grateful. Then read Mrs. Lambchop with JOY and excitement. “GEORGE!” should be a happy shout.

2
🎯 The 4 Steps
Work through these in order.
1️⃣

Read for accuracy. Read the passage out loud. Say every word correctly. Slow down where you need to.

1️⃣

Read for smoothness. Read it again. Let the sentences flow without pausing or stumbling.

2️⃣

Read with expression. Read it a third time. Let your voice show the feeling.

3️⃣

Record and submit. Use Canvas’s recording tool to record your final reading. Upload it to this week’s fluency assignment.

3
🤔 Self-Check
Before you submit your recording.
Ask yourself:

Did I say every word correctly?

Did my reading sound smooth?

Did my voice show the feeling of the passage?

🎙️

If you can answer “yes” to all three, you’re ready to submit. If not, read it once more.
